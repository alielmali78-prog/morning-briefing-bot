# PulseAI

AI-powered decision & action engine

---

## 🎯 What is PulseAI?

PulseAI is not a news bot.

It is a **daily decision system** that transforms information into:

- Insight
- Priority
- Action
- Opportunity

---

## 🔥 Example Output

🔥 Top Priority  
Priority Score: 20  
Priority Level: High  

⚡ Action Plan  
- Clarify 1 critical risk  
- Close 1 urgent decision today  
- Eliminate 1 distraction
